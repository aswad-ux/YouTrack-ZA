Privacy Policy & POPIA

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to YouTrack ZA (Pty) Ltd. ("Company," "we," "our," "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is collected, processed, and maintained in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of our website (youtrackza.com) and our telematics, vehicle tracking, and fleet management services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, ID number, or company registration details.
  • Contact Data: Email address, physical address, and telephone numbers.
  • Telematics Data: Real-time GPS location, vehicle speed, routes taken, ignition status, driver behavior, and dashcam footage (if applicable).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and usage data when you access our website or app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used for the following lawful purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our vehicle tracking and fleet management services.
  • To facilitate emergency response, armed SVR (Stolen Vehicle Recovery) dispatch, and liaise with SAPS or recovery partners in the event of theft or hijacking.
  • To generate automated SARS Tax Logbooks on your behalf.
  • To respond to your quote requests, inquiries, and provide customer support.
  • To comply with South African legal and regulatory obligations, including ICASA requirements.

3. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with:

  • Emergency Services & Law Enforcement: Including our 24/7 armed response partners and SAPS, purely for the purpose of recovering stolen vehicles or attending to emergencies.
  • Service Providers: Authorized third-party vendors who assist us with IT infrastructure, cloud hosting, and payment processing, all of whom are bound by strict confidentiality and POPIA compliance agreements.
  • Regulatory Bodies: When required by law or a valid subpoena.

4. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, and unauthorized disclosure. Our telematics platform utilizes end-to-end encryption to secure location and video data transmissions.

5. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject in South Africa, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request the correction, updating, or deletion of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Object to the processing of your personal information on reasonable grounds.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforeg@justice.gov.za).

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your rights, or have concerns about how we process your data, please contact our Information Officer:

  • Email: info@youtrackza.com
  • Website: youtrackza.com
