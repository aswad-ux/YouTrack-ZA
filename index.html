FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will installing the tracker void my vehicle's warranty?

No. Our hardware is ICASA approved and installed by certified local technicians using non-intrusive methods that preserve your vehicle's factory warranty.

How long does the fitment take?

Standard tracking unit fitment takes approximately 45-60 minutes. Advanced dashcam and video telematics systems may take up to 2 hours depending on the vehicle type.

Is the tracking unit completely hidden?

Yes. Our technicians conceal the core GPS tracking units deep within the vehicle's dashboard or chassis, making them extremely difficult for criminals to locate and tamper with.
